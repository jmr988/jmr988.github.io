About Me

I started my career in human resources before pivoting to technology, not because I stopped caring about people, but because I realized technology was one of the most powerful ways to help them. That shift led me to earn my B.S. in Computer Science at Aurora University while working as a Web Designer at Waubonsee Community College, where I maintain large-scale web infrastructure serving thousands of students and staff.

The work I am most proud of lives at the intersection of code and community. As Tech Lead and Board Member at La Tortuga Foundation I designed and deployed a full electronic medical records system for medical missions in rural Chiapas, Mexico, built to run on 8 tablets with no internet, in clinics serving patients who often have no other access to care. I have also volunteered as a Spanish interpreter for the foundation for six years, sitting across from patients and translating in moments that matter.

I build freelance WordPress projects for clients, contribute to open source health tech, and am always looking for the next problem worth solving, especially if the people it affects are usually overlooked by technology.
